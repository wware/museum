# Welcome to the Museum of Arcane Curiosities

A living, interactive collection of technical demonstrations, visualizations, and engineering explorations.

## The Museum Concept

This isn't a traditional documentation site or a static book. It's a **museum** — a curated collection of interactive exhibits that you can explore, manipulate, and learn from. Each "museum piece" demonstrates a concept through interaction rather than just description.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Interactive Physics Simulations** - Touch and manipulate systems to understand their behavior
- **System Architecture Visualizations** - See how distributed systems and infrastructure components interact
- **Live Code Demonstrations** - Execute and modify code snippets in real-time
- **Personal Notes & Bookmarks** - Your annotations are saved locally in your browser

## Featured Exhibits

### [Mass-Spring-Damper System](mass-spring.md)
An interactive physics simulation using Velocity Verlet integration. Drag the mass, adjust parameters, and watch energy conservation in action.

### [GitOps Cluster Visualizer](gitops-visualizer.md)
A D3-based visualization showing the state of distributed cluster nodes with interactive drill-down capabilities.

### [Orbits and the Space Elevator](orbital-mechanics.md)
Launch ships into orbit, find escape velocity by experiment, and release a climber from a space elevator to see when it escapes with no rocket at all.

## Using This Museum

- **Navigate**: Use the menu above to browse exhibits
- **Bookmark**: Click the bookmark button on any page to save it to your dashboard
- **Take Notes**: Each page has a notes area where you can jot down thoughts (saved in your browser)
- **Interact**: Click, drag, and modify the demonstrations to explore concepts deeply

## Philosophy

> "A printed book is the wrong artifact aimed at the wrong target audience."

Modern technical concepts — especially in areas like distributed systems, physics simulations, and interactive algorithms — can't be fully captured in static text. This museum embraces:

- **Searchability** over traditional indexes
- **Interaction** over passive reading
- **Living documents** over frozen snapshots
- **Personal annotation** over highlighters and margin notes
